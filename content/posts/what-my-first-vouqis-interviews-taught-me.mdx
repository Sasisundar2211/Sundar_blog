For months, Vouqis was a problem I worked on late at night while trying to figure out whether engineering teams needed protocol-level verification for MCP tools.

Then I spent a full day interviewing people who wanted to help build it.

I started every single conversation by stating the truth upfront: this was an unpaid, equity-based founding role. We were still validating the problem, speaking with engineering teams, and building the MVP.

Filtering for honesty immediately changed the room.

Some candidates had polished resumes but grew uncomfortable when there was no fixed playbook. Other candidates had less formal experience, yet asked sharper questions and showed genuine curiosity.

When a conversation hit a technical question neither of us knew how to answer, I watched how they reasoned through the uncertainty.

Early hiring is not about collecting impressive resumes. It is about finding people who care about the same problem and know how to think when the path is unclear.
